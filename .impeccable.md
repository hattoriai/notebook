## Design Context

### Users
People who want to write, share, and revisit rich documents. Notebook is also a runnable demonstration of Kotoba's full feature set and a useful app in its own right.

### Brand Personality
Calm, thoughtful, editorial. Writing and reading should feel unhurried.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light paper and ink, restrained warm accents, generous margins, typographic hierarchy, and unobtrusive navigation.

### Design Principles
- Keep the document central and controls legible.
- Make writing, collaboration, and media feel like parts of one document.
- Use plain language and clear feedback for save and upload states.
- Support narrow screens and keyboard use without hiding essential actions.
